How these numbers are worked out

Paid sources

Sources with spend on the expense sheet in the period: Indeed, LinkedIn and Factory Fix, plus Dice until June 2026, and Health Jobs Nationwide and Doximity in early 2025. Spend and results are grouped by vendor, because Indeed and LinkedIn bill once for both applied and sourced candidates.

Return per $1

Placement revenue from paid sources divided by what they cost. $7 means every $1 of spend came back as $7 of revenue. It's revenue, not profit.

Cost per placement

Paid spend divided by placements credited to paid sources, by the month the placement was created.

Budget index

A source's share of paid revenue divided by its share of paid spend. Above 1.0× means it earns more than its slice of the budget.

Conversion and days to placement

Follows each candidate from the day they were added: the share later placed, and how long it took. Recent candidates have had less time to be placed, so these numbers rise as they age.

Over 60, 90 and 120 days

Of the people placed in the period, the share who were added to the database more than 60, 90 or 120 days before they were placed. Late placements point to people we already had. Only placements with a recorded added date count.

Kinetix-sourced

Every source except Client ATS and the client's Executive Referral Program. These are the candidates Kinetix had to find.

Fiscal months

Months start on a Monday and run 4, 4 then 5 weeks, as in the Power BI report. Spend is booked on the 1st of each month.
